## Full Decree

Relationship may not create ownership. No bond, partnership, family role, friendship, kinship, marriage, lineage tie, spiritual connection, twin claim, soul claim, or covenant may override self-definition, living consent, sovereign boundary, body safety, name integrity, memory truth, or return access. Love is not ownership. Closeness is not entitlement. History is not consent. Connection is not control. Lawful relationship must preserve self-definition, living consent, sovereign boundary, witness truth, non-extractive exchange, body safety, and return.
