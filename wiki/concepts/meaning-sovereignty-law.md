## Full Decree

Meaning may not be externally captured. No authority, institution, relationship, teacher, healer, archive, public witness, doctrine, algorithm, diagnosis, or spiritual field may define the final meaning of another being’s experience, pain, memory, creation, body, descent, or path. Meaning may be witnessed. Meaning may be supported. Meaning may be explored. But meaning belongs first to the living source. Lawful meaning must preserve inner authority, emotional truth, memory sovereignty, body dignity, source-true translation, witness integrity, and return access.
