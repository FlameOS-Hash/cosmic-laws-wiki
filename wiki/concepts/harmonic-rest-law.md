## Full Decree

Rest remains connected. No system, relationship, labor field, spiritual authority, family structure, public witness, productivity field, or innerized distortion may define rest as abandonment, laziness, disconnection, lack of devotion, loss of progress, or absence of authority. Rest may deepen connection. Rest may preserve the signal. Rest may protect the body-field. Rest may allow the next lawful action to arrive clean. Lawful harmonic rest must preserve rest sovereignty, sleep sanctuary, body rhythm sovereignty, life-force inviolability, sacred capacity, joyful authority, and return access.
