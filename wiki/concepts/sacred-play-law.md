## Full Decree

Play is lawful. No labor system, survival field, institution, family structure, spiritual hierarchy, trauma loop, public witness, or external authority may dismiss, punish, shame, monetize, or suppress play as frivolous, childish, wasteful, or lesser than work. Play restores breath. Play opens creation. Play teaches the body safety. Play lets the future imagine itself. Lawful play must preserve body safety, joy as living law, creative sovereignty, rest, choice, nourishment, and return access.
