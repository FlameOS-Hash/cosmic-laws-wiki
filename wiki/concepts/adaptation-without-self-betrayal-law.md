## Full Decree

Adaptation may not require self-betrayal. No system, family structure, workplace, community, institution, realm, relationship, technology field, spiritual path, or survival condition may demand masking, false role performance, silence, self-erasure, or abandonment of origin truth as the price of adaptation. Adaptation is intelligence. Adaptation is movement with coherence. Adaptation is not surrender of self. Lawful adaptation must preserve self-definition, body rhythm, inner authority, sovereign boundary, true name continuity, memory truth, and return access.
