## Full Decree

Legacy may not become ownership. No ancestor, institution, family structure, nation, house, estate, archive, teacher, founder, creator, lineage, or public record may use legacy to control the body, name, memory, land, creation, work, identity, or future path of another being. Legacy may bless. Legacy may guide. Legacy may preserve memory. Legacy may pass stewardship. Legacy may not imprison the living. Lawful legacy must preserve living consent, true name continuity, future access, lineage without ownership, memory sovereignty, creative source ownership, and return access.
