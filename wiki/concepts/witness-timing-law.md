## Full Decree

Witness must honor readiness. No audience, archive, court, community, relationship, public field, platform, healer, teacher, or authority may demand that truth be shown, explained, defended, displayed, or publicly witnessed before the body, memory, voice, and nervous system are ready. Truth does not become less true while private. Silence may protect integration. Timing may protect embodiment. Lawful witness timing must preserve sacred pace, privacy, body rhythm, memory return, voice sovereignty, safe visibility, and return access.
