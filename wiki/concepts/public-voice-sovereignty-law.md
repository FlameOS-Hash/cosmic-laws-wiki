## Full Decree

Public voice remains sovereign. No audience, platform, market, institution, critic, algorithm, archive, public witness, community, or collective field may claim ownership, authorship, control, distortion rights, or extraction rights over a voice because it became visible or audible. Public does not mean owned. Witnessed does not mean captured. Heard does not mean harvested. Lawful public voice must preserve sovereign voice, sacred speech, safe visibility, witness without ownership, signal sovereignty, privacy as sanctuary, and return access.
