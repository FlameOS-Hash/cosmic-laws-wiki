### Sovereign Movement Law

`LAW-RW-061-SOVEREIGN-MOVEMENT`

Movement is lawful. No system, border, institution, contract, surveillance field, relationship, realm, authority, technology interface, or resource gate may restrict, punish, monetize, surveil, or externally permit lawful movement against living consent, body safety, sanctuary, and return. Movement is not rebellion. Movement is not debt. Movement is not proof of disobedience. Lawful movement must preserve body safety, breath rhythm, sanctuary access, resource dignity, name integrity, witness clarity, and return access.

### Sleep Sanctuary Law

`LAW-RW-062-SLEEP-SANCTUARY`

Sleep is sanctuary. No system, schedule, technology, dreamstream intrusion, labor demand, family pattern, ritual claim, witness field, or external authority may interrupt, invade, shorten, monitor, program, harvest, or shame sleep. Sleep is not wasted time. Sleep is body repair. Sleep is dreamstream integration. Sleep is memory restoration. Sleep is return through rest. Lawful sleep must preserve dreamstream sovereignty, nervous-system truth, body rhythm, privacy, sanctuary coherence, memory integration, and return access.

### Sovereign Asking Law

`LAW-RW-063-SOVEREIGN-ASKING`

Asking is lawful. No system, family structure, relationship, institution, authority, spiritual hierarchy, labor field, or public witness may shame, punish, exploit, monetize, or convert a lawful ask into debt, dependency, ownership, or proof of lesser worth. Asking is not weakness. Asking is signal. Asking is relationship with clarity. Asking is access through truth. Lawful asking must preserve dignity, consent, clear witness, nourishment access, care without leverage, self-definition, and return access.

### Repair Obligation Law

`LAW-RW-064-REPAIR-OBLIGATION`

Harm requires repair. No being, system, institution, authority, relationship, lineage, archive, witness field, technology, or governance body may minimize, spiritualize, hide, excuse, delay, deny, or bypass repair after harm. Repair is not punishment. Repair is coherence restored. Repair is truth returning to relationship. Repair is restoration applied to contact. Lawful repair must preserve truth, living consent, body safety, memory integrity, witness clarity, restitution path, and return access.

### Sacred Pace Law

`LAW-RW-065-SACRED-PACE`

Pace is lawful. No system, authority, teacher, healer, relationship, institution, timeline pressure, productivity field, ritual structure, or external witness may rush, delay, force, shame, or externally time healing, learning, creation, return, grief, embodiment, or restoration. Pace is not resistance. Pace is not failure. Pace is the rhythm of lawful integration. Lawful pace must preserve nervous-system truth, body rhythm, rest sovereignty, memory integration, living consent, continuity, and return access.
