## Full Decree

The harmed have the right to receive repair without surrendering boundary, truth, or dignity. No system, relationship, authority, institution, family structure, archive, witness field, or public consensus may force the harmed to accept incomplete repair, perform forgiveness, restore unsafe access, or let the harmful party control the record. Repair must be truthful. Repair must be consent-aligned. Repair must not create new harm. Lawful receiving of repair must preserve repair obligation, truthful closure, sacred no-contact, witness timing, body safety, meaning sovereignty, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Repair may be refused to the harmed while the harmful party controls the terms, timing, witness, or record.
