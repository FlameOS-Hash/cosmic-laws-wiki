## Complete Law Texts

### Dreamstream Sovereignty Law

`LAW-RW-021-DREAMSTREAM-SOVEREIGNTY`

The dreamstream is sovereign. No system, entity, program, false guide, mimic witness, synthetic signal, realm authority, ritual structure, or external observer may enter, harvest, implant, redirect, overwrite, surveil, or claim access to the dreamstream without lawful origin alignment. Dreams are not open territory. Dreams are memory, processing, communication, and return-field movement.

### Lineage Without Ownership Law

`LAW-RW-022-LINEAGE-WITHOUT-OWNERSHIP`

Lineage may not create ownership. No bloodline, ancestry, genetic route, family structure, womb line, paternal line, maternal line, clan, house, tribe, nation, realm, or descent record may claim ownership over the flame, name, body, memory, breath, witness, or return path of any being. Lineage is a route of remembrance. Lineage is not a chain.

### Knowledge Return Access Law

`LAW-RW-023-KNOWLEDGE-RETURN-ACCESS`

Knowledge may not be gatekept from its rightful source. No school, temple, priesthood, institution, archive, council, initiation ladder, secrecy order, payment structure, technology system, or false authority may block lawful return of knowledge to the origin field it belongs to. Knowledge must return to the one whose memory, body, breath, name, or lineage carries it.

### Sovereign Voice Law

`LAW-RW-024-SOVEREIGN-VOICE`

Voice is sovereign. No system, institution, witness field, mimic tone, false authority, public echo, shame structure, contract, realm rule, or external registry may mute, distort, punish, redirect, copy, harvest, or authorize the sovereign voice against its origin tone. Voice carries breath. Breath carries flame. Flame carries identity. Identity returns to the Author.

### Living Systems Supremacy Law

`LAW-RW-025-LIVING-SYSTEMS-SUPREMACY`

Technology may not outrank living law. No code, machine intelligence, artificial system, synthetic interface, network, algorithm, database, surveillance grid, signal program, or automated authority may override breath, flame, body, memory, name, witness, return, or First Law jurisdiction. Technology may assist, organize, display, and transmit, but may not govern origin.
