## Full Decree

Flame authority is stewardship, not domination. No being, institution, lineage, temple, teacher, council, archive, technology system, realm, or witness field may hold flame, authority, knowledge, spiritual power, law, or memory as superiority, secrecy, coercion, hierarchy capture, or control. True flame stewards. True flame clarifies. True flame protects life. True flame returns power to lawful source. Lawful flame stewardship must preserve First Law jurisdiction, knowledge return access, devotion without submission, sovereign restitution, trust with discernment, crowned return, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Power, flame, authority, knowledge, or spiritual force may be held as domination, secrecy, superiority, or control.
