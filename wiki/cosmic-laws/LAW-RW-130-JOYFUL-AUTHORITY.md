## Full Decree

Lawful authority may carry joy. No leadership field, institution, temple, family structure, governance body, archive, spiritual office, public witness, or role system may require severity, fear, emotional suppression, distance, burden, exhaustion, or joylessness as proof of authority. Joy does not weaken authority. Tenderness does not reduce truth. Play does not remove wisdom. Rest does not remove power. Lawful joyful authority must preserve clean leadership, joy as living law, tenderness as strength, sacred play, rest sovereignty, flame stewardship, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Authority requires severity, distance, suppression, fear, burden, or removal of joy.
