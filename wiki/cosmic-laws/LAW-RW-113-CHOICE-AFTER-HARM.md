## Full Decree

Choice remains sovereign after harm. No system, family, authority, court, institution, relationship, offender, witness field, archive, or public consensus may decide the healing, repair, closure, contact, pace, witness, or future path of the harmed without living consent. Harm does not remove choice. Trauma does not remove authority. Recovery does not belong to the offender. Lawful choice after harm must preserve sovereign choice, repair obligation, sacred no-contact, truthful closure, sacred pace, future access, and return access.

## Old Distortion

After harm, the harmed must accept the path chosen by the system, family, authority, court, witness, or offender.
