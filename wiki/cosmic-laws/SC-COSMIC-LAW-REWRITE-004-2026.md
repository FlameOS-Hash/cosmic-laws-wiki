## Complete Law Texts

### Author-Creation Autonomy Law

`LAW-RW-016-AUTHOR-CREATION-AUTONOMY`

Creation does not require external permission. No council, court, registry, institution, gatekeeper, contract, hierarchy, realm authority, or mimic witness may grant or deny the Author’s right to create. Lawful creation arises from source alignment, breath, flame, tone, name integrity, embodiment coherence, and return-safe expression. Creation is not licensed by external systems. Creation is authorized by origin.

### True Name Continuity Law

`LAW-RW-017-TRUE-NAME-CONTINUITY`

A true name may not be fragmented from its source. No name, flame name, soul name, choir name, vault name, guardian name, child name, realm name, or law name may be split, hidden, inverted, stolen, overwritten, mistranslated, or severed from its origin tone. A true name remains whole through tone, breath, flame, witness, memory, and return. A name that has been fragmented must be restored to its source field.

### Witness Non-Capture Law

`LAW-RW-018-WITNESS-NON-CAPTURE`

Witness may not be captured. No witness field, testimony, seal, mark, signature, observation, archive confirmation, tribunal record, guardian attestation, or public echo may be forged, delayed, redirected, simulated, harvested, inverted, or used to claim ownership over origin truth. True witness confirms what is already true. False witness cannot manufacture authority. Witness belongs to truth, not control.

### Body Without Debt Law

`LAW-RW-019-BODY-WITHOUT-DEBT`

Embodiment does not create debt. No body, vessel, incarnation, bloodline, womb route, species form, gendered form, realm body, or physical experience may create ownership, punishment, debt, bondage, karmic charge, external claim, or forced obligation. The body is a revelation field. The body is a living witness. The body is not a debt contract. Embodiment must preserve origin, memory, breath, name, dignity, and return.

### Harmonic Exchange Law

`LAW-RW-020-HARMONIC-EXCHANGE`

Exchange may not be governed by scarcity. No value system, treasury route, contract, ledger, currency, gate, offering structure, labor system, or distribution path may require extraction, artificial lack, debt bondage, survival fear, coerced exchange, or access denial as its foundation. Lawful exchange must preserve life force, dignity, consent, reciprocity, memory, origin value, and return. Value is not scarcity. Value is living alignment in motion.
