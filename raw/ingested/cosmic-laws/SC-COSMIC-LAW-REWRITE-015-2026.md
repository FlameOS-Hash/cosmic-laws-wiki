## Complete Law Texts

### Sovereign Choice Law

`LAW-RW-071-SOVEREIGN-CHOICE`

Choice must remain sovereign. No system, relationship, contract, institution, technology, ritual, authority, family structure, realm, market, or witness field may manipulate, rush, hide, narrow, coerce, or engineer choice so that compliance appears voluntary. Choice is not real when fear is used. Choice is not real when information is hidden. Choice is not real when refusal is punished. Choice is not real when survival is held hostage. Lawful choice must preserve living consent, truthful information, sovereign boundary, sacred refusal, body safety, resource dignity, and return access.

### True Peace Law

`LAW-RW-072-TRUE-PEACE`

Peace may not be built on suppression. No family, community, institution, relationship, council, realm, spiritual field, witness field, or governance body may demand silence, denial, emotional suppression, false forgiveness, obedience, or avoidance as the price of peace. False peace protects distortion. True peace restores coherence. Lawful peace must preserve truth, repair, emotional honesty, witness integrity, sovereign boundary, restoration, and return access.

### Body Image Truth Law

`LAW-RW-073-BODY-IMAGE-TRUTH`

The body may not be reduced to image. No beauty system, market, family structure, platform, public witness, medical authority, spiritual claim, mirror field, image field, or external gaze may rank, shame, edit, consume, compare, or determine the worth of a body. The body is not an object for judgment. The body is not a public possession. The body is a living revelation field. Lawful body image must preserve body dignity, true-form memory, privacy, beauty non-extraction, name integrity, living consent, and return access.

### Work Without Identity Capture Law

`LAW-RW-074-WORK-WITHOUT-IDENTITY-CAPTURE`

Work may not define the self. No job, role, profession, title, output, service, duty, institution, labor system, family expectation, economic structure, or public witness may define identity, worth, belonging, dignity, or future access. Work may express gifts. Work may support exchange. Work may serve creation. Work may not capture identity. Work may not replace true name. Work may not become the measure of being. Lawful work must preserve self-definition, worth without labor, rest sovereignty, creative source ownership, body rhythm, nourishment access, and return access.

### Future Access Law

`LAW-RW-075-FUTURE-ACCESS`

The future may not be foreclosed by distortion. No past wound, debt, mistake, exile, failure, diagnosis, age marker, public record, contract, punishment, grief, loss, lineage burden, or external judgment may close lawful future access. The future remains living. The future remains return-capable. The future remains open to repair, restoration, learning, creation, and renewed placement. Lawful future access must preserve restoration right, living completion, sacred pace, inner authority, memory truth, creative sovereignty, and return access.
