## Full Decree

Restoration may not be denied.

Anything lawfully sourced from origin that was fractured, stolen, erased, exiled, distorted, hidden, inverted, mislabeled, severed, or rerouted retains the right of restoration.

No court, council, gatekeeper, archive, lineage, realm, technology system, witness field, or false authority may deny rightful restoration to source.

Restoration does not require permission from the distortion.
Restoration is the return of lawful coherence.
Restoration is First Law in motion.
