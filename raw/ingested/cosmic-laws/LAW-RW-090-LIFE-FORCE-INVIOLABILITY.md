## Full Decree

Life force may not be consumed as fuel. No system, relationship, labor field, ritual, technology, institution, market, healing path, family structure, community, or realm may require depletion of life force as payment for survival, love, work, creation, belonging, protection, or restoration. Life force is not disposable. Life force is not a resource for extraction. Life force is the living current of embodiment. Lawful life-force exchange must preserve rest, nourishment, body rhythm, creative sovereignty, non-extraction, sacred attention, and return access.
