## Complete Law Texts

### Sacred Memory Witness Law

`LAW-RW-141-SACRED-MEMORY-WITNESS`

Memory is lawful before external proof. No archive, institution, witness field, family system, court, platform, relationship, healer, teacher, public record, or external authority may require memory to be proven, displayed, defended, or publicly validated before it may be trusted, honored, restored, or integrated by the living source. Memory may seek witness. Memory may receive confirmation. Memory may be clarified through records. Memory may unfold in sacred timing. But memory does not belong to the witness. Lawful memory witness must preserve memory sovereignty, memory return, memory integration, sacred record context, witness without ownership, embodied truth, and return access.

### Sovereign Embodiment Law

`LAW-RW-142-SOVEREIGN-EMBODIMENT`

Embodiment is lawful authority in form. No system, doctrine, spiritual hierarchy, institution, lineage, realm, family structure, public witness, or false authority may define the body as lesser than spirit, separate from source, unworthy of authority, or evidence of exile. The body is not a prison. The body is not punishment. The body is not proof of separation. The body is breath in form. The body is memory in motion. The body is temple, witness, instrument, sanctuary, and living gate. Lawful embodiment must preserve body without debt, living temple, embodied truth, body pace, descent without exile, identity before form, and return access.

### Harmonic World-Action Law

`LAW-RW-143-HARMONIC-WORLD-ACTION`

World-action may remain source-aligned. No institution, market, platform, governance body, labor field, public witness, community, relationship, technology system, or survival structure may require the living source to dilute truth, abandon inner authority, submit to extraction, accept distortion, or compromise origin law in order to act, build, teach, create, lead, serve, publish, repair, or restore in the world. Action may be practical without becoming false. Visibility may be public without becoming owned. Service may be generous without becoming depletion. Leadership may be active without becoming domination. Lawful world-action must preserve clean leadership, public voice sovereignty, creative source ownership, clean power, resource truth, field integrity, and return access.

### Final Return Completion Law

`LAW-RW-144-FINAL-RETURN-COMPLETION`

Return is complete when source truth is restored. No system, realm, archive, witness field, institution, lineage, timeline, gate, authority, public consensus, spiritual hierarchy, or external registry may keep return partial, deferred, conditional, fragmented, uncrowned, or permission-gated once lawful source truth, name, memory, body, voice, authority, and path have returned to alignment. Return is not a request. Return is not a future debt. Return is not external permission. Return is source truth coming home. Lawful final return must preserve crowned return, sovereign continuity, future access, living completion, original blessing, clean power, and full return access.
