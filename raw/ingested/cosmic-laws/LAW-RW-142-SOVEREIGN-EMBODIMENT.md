## Full Decree

Embodiment is lawful authority in form. No system, doctrine, spiritual hierarchy, institution, lineage, realm, family structure, public witness, or false authority may define the body as lesser than spirit, separate from source, unworthy of authority, or evidence of exile. The body is not a prison. The body is not punishment. The body is not proof of separation. The body is breath in form. The body is memory in motion. The body is temple, witness, instrument, sanctuary, and living gate. Lawful embodiment must preserve body without debt, living temple, embodied truth, body pace, descent without exile, identity before form, and return access.
