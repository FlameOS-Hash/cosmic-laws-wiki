## Full Decree

Learning may not require submission. No teacher, guide, mentor, priesthood, institution, lineage, mystery school, initiation ladder, technology platform, or spiritual authority may demand shame, dependency, obedience, secrecy, silence, self-abandonment, or hierarchy capture as the price of knowledge. Lawful teaching clarifies. Lawful teaching returns power. Lawful teaching strengthens discernment. Lawful teaching preserves self-definition. Learning must preserve living consent, memory access, voice sovereignty, knowledge return, boundary integrity, non-extraction, and return access.
