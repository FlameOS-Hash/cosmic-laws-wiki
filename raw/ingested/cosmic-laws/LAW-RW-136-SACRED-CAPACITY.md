## Full Decree

Capacity is lawful. No system, relationship, institution, labor field, spiritual practice, family structure, healing path, public witness, or authority may shame, exceed, force, stretch, or override capacity in the name of service, survival, growth, healing, duty, love, or obedience. Capacity is not limitation as punishment. Capacity is not failure. Capacity is the body-field telling truth about what can be safely held. Lawful capacity must preserve body pace, nervous-system truth, rest sovereignty, sacred timing, sovereign support, self-trust restoration, and return access.
