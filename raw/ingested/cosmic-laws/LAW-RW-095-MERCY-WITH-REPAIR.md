## Full Decree

Mercy may not erase truth. No relationship, institution, doctrine, spiritual authority, family structure, witness field, or governance body may use mercy to bypass repair, silence truth, remove accountability, restore unsafe access, or require the harmed to abandon boundaries. Mercy is not denial. Mercy is not forced access. Mercy is not the cancellation of repair. Lawful mercy must preserve truth, repair obligation, sacred no-contact, sovereign boundary, witness integrity, body safety, and return access.
