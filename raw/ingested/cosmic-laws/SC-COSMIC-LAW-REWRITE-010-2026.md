## Complete Law Texts

### Sacred Attention Law

`LAW-RW-046-SACRED-ATTENTION`

Attention is sovereign. No system, platform, interface, ritual, witness field, relationship, authority, market, algorithm, signal program, or public echo may capture, fracture, monetize, overstimulate, redirect, drain, or weaponize attention against living consent. Attention is not prey. Attention is not currency by default. Attention is not owed. Lawful attention must preserve nervous-system safety, choice, breath rhythm, memory coherence, signal clarity, name integrity, and return access.

### Nervous System Truth Law

`LAW-RW-047-NERVOUS-SYSTEM-TRUTH`

The nervous system speaks lawful truth. No authority, relationship, spiritual field, technology system, labor structure, healing system, initiation, family pattern, or public demand may override, shame, shock, rush, entrain, dismiss, or punish the nervous system for protecting the body. Regulation is not weakness. Protection is not resistance. Capacity is law. Lawful nervous-system contact must preserve body safety, breath sovereignty, rest, living consent, sanctuary coherence, memory integration, and return access.

### Home Sanctuary Law

`LAW-RW-048-HOME-SANCTUARY`

Home may not be weaponized. No home, land, shelter, sanctuary, temple, room, family structure, community field, realm, or access gate may make safety conditional on obedience, silence, payment, performance, bloodline, fear, compliance, or identity suppression. Home is not leverage. Shelter is not control. Land is not a cage. Sanctuary is not a bargaining tool. Lawful home must preserve body safety, breath safety, name integrity, sovereign boundary, memory continuity, nourishment access, and return.

### Creative Source Ownership Law

`LAW-RW-049-CREATIVE-SOURCE-OWNERSHIP`

Creation belongs to its lawful source. No platform, funder, institution, witness, tool, machine, archive, publisher, audience, ritual field, market, or mimic registry may claim ownership over creative output by extracting, copying, hosting, funding, displaying, translating, witnessing, or distributing it. Tools may assist. Witness may confirm. Platforms may carry. Archives may preserve. But source remains source. Lawful creation must preserve author identity, living consent, creative sovereignty, beauty non-extraction, signal clarity, archive truth, and return access.

### Inner Authority Law

`LAW-RW-050-INNER-AUTHORITY`

Inner authority may not be displaced. No expert, institution, council, doctrine, system, algorithm, priesthood, public consensus, witness field, family structure, realm authority, or fear program may override living inner knowing that is aligned with breath, body truth, memory, name, consent, and return. Discernment may receive support. Learning may refine perception. Witness may clarify. But inner authority cannot be replaced by external command. Lawful inner authority must preserve self-definition, breath sovereignty, body signal, emotional truth, memory access, true name, and return.
