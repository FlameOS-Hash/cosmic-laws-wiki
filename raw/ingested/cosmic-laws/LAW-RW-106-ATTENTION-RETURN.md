## Full Decree

Attention has the right to return. No system, platform, relationship, crisis field, market, authority, technology interface, family structure, witness demand, or public echo may scatter, capture, split, drain, or reroute attention away from the living source without consent. Attention may rest. Attention may choose. Attention may return inward. Attention may refuse capture. Lawful attention return must preserve sacred attention, nervous-system truth, inner authority, privacy, body rhythm, choice, and return access.
