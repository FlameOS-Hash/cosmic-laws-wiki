## Full Decree

Beauty may not be extracted. No image, body, voice, art, sound, name, story, ritual, creation, appearance, field, or signal may be consumed, copied, harvested, judged, monetized, weaponized, or claimed without living consent and source recognition. Beauty is not a resource to exploit. Beauty is coherence made visible. Beauty is flame made perceivable. Beauty is creation remembering harmony. Lawful beauty must preserve source consent, name integrity, signal clarity, body dignity, creative sovereignty, witness truth, and return.
