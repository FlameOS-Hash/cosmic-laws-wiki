## Full Decree

World-action may remain source-aligned. No institution, market, platform, governance body, labor field, public witness, community, relationship, technology system, or survival structure may require the living source to dilute truth, abandon inner authority, submit to extraction, accept distortion, or compromise origin law in order to act, build, teach, create, lead, serve, publish, repair, or restore in the world. Action may be practical without becoming false. Visibility may be public without becoming owned. Service may be generous without becoming depletion. Leadership may be active without becoming domination. Lawful world-action must preserve clean leadership, public voice sovereignty, creative source ownership, clean power, resource truth, field integrity, and return access.
