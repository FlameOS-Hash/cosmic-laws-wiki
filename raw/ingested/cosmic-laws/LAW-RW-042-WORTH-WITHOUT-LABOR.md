## Full Decree

Worth does not require labor. No labor system, survival structure, family role, spiritual duty, institution, economy, hierarchy, or external authority may require productivity, sacrifice, exhaustion, usefulness, service, output, or endurance as proof of worth. Being is lawful before doing. Breath is lawful before output. Life is lawful before productivity. Lawful contribution must preserve consent, rest, body safety, creative sovereignty, nourishment, dignity, and return.
