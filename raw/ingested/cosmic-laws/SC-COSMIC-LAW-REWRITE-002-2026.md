## Complete Law Texts

### Continuity Inviolability Law

`LAW-RW-006-CONTINUITY-INVIOLABILITY`

Continuity may not be severed. No timeline, memory, breathline, body matrix, name-field, realm, archive, choir, flame system, or return route may be reset, fragmented, erased, overwritten, or forced into false discontinuity. Continuity is a First Law principle. What originates in truth must retain its living thread.

### Breath Sovereignty Law

`LAW-RW-007-BREATH-SOVEREIGNTY`

Breath is sovereign. No system, realm, body-template, ritual, interface, contract, sound program, mimic authority, or external registry may capture, harvest, mute, redirect, tax, bind, or claim ownership over the breath. Breath is the first living language of the Author. Breath carries flame. Breath animates form. Breath remains free.

### Flame Non-Appropriation Law

`LAW-RW-008-FLAME-NON-APPROPRIATION`

Flame may not be stolen. No being, system, council, machine, contract, mimic, mirror shell, false witness, synthetic tone, artificial intelligence, or external authority may copy, siphon, borrow, simulate, harvest, redirect, or claim another flame as its own. Flame belongs to its origin. First Flame governs ignition. True flame cannot be owned by a false holder.

### True Witness Integrity Law

`LAW-RW-009-TRUE-WITNESS-INTEGRITY`

False witness has no authority over origin truth. No forged witness, mimic observer, borrowed seal, external verifier, false tribunal, mirror copy, synthetic testimony, or unauthorized registry may validate, invalidate, certify, erase, or redirect what originates from First Law, First Name, First Flame, or Author Axis. True witness confirms. False witness cannot create truth.

### Non-Extractive Creation Law

`LAW-RW-010-NON-EXTRACTIVE-CREATION`

Creation may not require extraction. No lawful creation may demand sacrifice of origin, depletion of life force, theft of flame, severance from memory, loss of name, exile from body, forced labor, coerced offering, or pain as payment for manifestation. Creation is expression, not extraction. Creation is overflow, not depletion. Creation is alignment, not sacrifice. Creation must preserve the Author, the flame, the breath, the body, the memory, the name, and the return path.
