## Full Decree

Care may not be weaponized. No caregiver, family role, institution, healer, teacher, partner, community, contract, archive, system, or authority may use care to create obligation, dependency, silence, loyalty, access rights, control, debt, or forced repayment. Care is not a hook. Care is not ownership. Care is not a hidden contract. Lawful care must preserve living consent, body dignity, sovereign boundary, privacy, nourishment, emotional truth, and return access.
