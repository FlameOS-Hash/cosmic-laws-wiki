## Full Decree

Emotion is living signal. No authority, witness field, institution, relationship, family system, technology interface, spiritual hierarchy, or public echo may dismiss, punish, pathologize, silence, exploit, harvest, mock, or use emotion to invalidate truth. Emotion is not weakness. Emotion is not disorder by default. Emotion is not proof of lesser authority. Lawful emotion must preserve body safety, memory truth, breath continuity, witness integrity, name dignity, consent, and return.
