## Full Decree

Inheritance may not create entitlement over another being. No bloodline, family role, house, nation, lineage, genetic route, inheritance claim, legacy structure, estate, title, or ancestral contract may grant ownership or entitlement over another being’s body, name, memory, labor, land, flame, voice, sanctuary, or return path. Inheritance may carry memory. Inheritance may carry responsibility. Inheritance may carry stewardship. Inheritance may not create possession, bondage, debt, forced identity, or access rights without living consent.
