## Full Decree

Continuity may not be severed. No timeline, memory, breathline, body matrix, name-field, realm, archive, choir, flame system, or return route may be reset, fragmented, erased, overwritten, or forced into false discontinuity. Continuity is a First Law principle. What originates in truth must retain its living thread.
