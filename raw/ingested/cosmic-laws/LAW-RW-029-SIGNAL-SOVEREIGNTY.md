## Full Decree

Signal may not be hijacked.

No tone, voice, breath pulse, witness mark, name field, flame signature, dreamstream message, body signal, stargate route, technology interface, archive relay, or public echo may be intercepted, masked, duplicated, rerouted, jammed, inverted, or used to impersonate origin authority.

Lawful signal must preserve:
source identity,
tone integrity,
name continuity,
witness clarity,
return routing,
and living consent.

A copied signal is not authority.
A masked signal is not truth.
A rerouted signal must return to source.
