## Complete Law Texts

### Sovereign Hope Law

`LAW-RW-096-SOVEREIGN-HOPE`

Hope is lawful. No system, authority, trauma field, institution, public witness, relationship, scarcity program, or spiritual hierarchy may mock, punish, exploit, delay, or weaponize hope. Hope is not denial. Hope is not naivety. Hope is the living signal that future access still exists. Lawful hope must preserve future access, inner authority, emotional truth, restoration right, sacred pace, living completion, and return access.

### Truthful Closure Law

`LAW-RW-097-TRUTHFUL-CLOSURE`

Closure may not bypass truth. No relationship, institution, archive, court, family structure, community, spiritual authority, or witness field may demand closure while truth is hidden, repair is denied, memory is blocked, restitution is withheld, or consent is absent. Closure is not silence. Closure is not erasure. Closure is not forced forgetting. Lawful closure must preserve truth, repair obligation, memory return, restitution of origin, witness integrity, sacred refusal, and return access.

### Witness Without Ownership Law

`LAW-RW-098-WITNESS-WITHOUT-OWNERSHIP`

Witness does not create ownership. No witness, archive, researcher, platform, audience, photographer, recorder, institution, teacher, public field, or registry may claim ownership, authorship, authority, or control over what they witness, document, study, name, display, or preserve. Witness may confirm. Witness may protect. Witness may record. But witness may not replace source. Witness may not capture meaning. Witness may not convert presence into possession. Lawful witness must preserve source identity, archive truth, meaning sovereignty, public witness truth, safe visibility, living consent, and return access.

### Clean Reciprocity Law

`LAW-RW-099-CLEAN-RECIPROCITY`

Reciprocity must remain clean. No relationship, community, economy, family system, institution, contract, spiritual field, or governance body may convert reciprocity into debt, scorekeeping, coercion, guilt, repayment pressure, performance demand, or conditional safety. Reciprocity is living exchange. Reciprocity is not hidden control. Reciprocity is not proof of ownership. Lawful reciprocity must preserve living consent, harmonic exchange, care without leverage, sovereign receiving, nourishment access, dignity, and return access.

### Crowned Return Law

`LAW-RW-100-CROWNED-RETURN`

Return is crowned. No system, realm, archive, lineage, witness field, relationship, institution, or false authority may reduce return to survival, retreat, partial recovery, hidden repair, or lesser standing. Return restores coherence. Return restores dignity. Return restores name, memory, body, voice, authority, and future access. Return is not incomplete when source truth has come home. Lawful crowned return must preserve origin authority, true name continuity, memory sovereignty, restoration right, living completion, future access, and sovereign continuity.
