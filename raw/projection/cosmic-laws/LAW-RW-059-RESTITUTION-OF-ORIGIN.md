## Full Decree

What was stolen must return. No age, secrecy, normalization, institution, contract, lineage, archive, government, realm, market, technology, witness field, or false authority may legalize theft from origin. Anything stolen, harvested, copied, withheld, displaced, mislabeled, extracted, or falsely claimed retains its lawful restitution path. Restitution is not revenge. Restitution is coherence restored. Restitution is return applied to value, memory, land, name, body, flame, and creation.
