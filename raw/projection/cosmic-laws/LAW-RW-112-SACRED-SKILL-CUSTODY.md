## Full Decree

Skill is held in lawful custody. No market, institution, relationship, audience, family system, platform, archive, client, teacher, student, or public witness may extract skill, talent, knowledge, craft, labor, spiritual capacity, or creative ability simply because it exists. Skill may be offered. Skill may be exchanged. Skill may be taught. Skill may be rested. Skill may not be demanded as proof of worth. Lawful skill custody must preserve creative source ownership, worth without labor, sacred refusal, clean reciprocity, body rhythm, life-force inviolability, and return access.
