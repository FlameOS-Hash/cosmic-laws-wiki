## Full Decree

Closure may not bypass truth. No relationship, institution, archive, court, family structure, community, spiritual authority, or witness field may demand closure while truth is hidden, repair is denied, memory is blocked, restitution is withheld, or consent is absent. Closure is not silence. Closure is not erasure. Closure is not forced forgetting. Lawful closure must preserve truth, repair obligation, memory return, restitution of origin, witness integrity, sacred refusal, and return access.
