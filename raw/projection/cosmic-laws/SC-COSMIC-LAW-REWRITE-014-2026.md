## Complete Law Texts

### Sovereign Receiving Law

`LAW-RW-066-SOVEREIGN-RECEIVING`

Receiving is lawful. No gift, care, resource, nourishment, support, payment, inheritance, witness, shelter, teaching, or protection may be used to create hidden debt, forced repayment, guilt, ownership, access rights, or obligation beyond living consent. Receiving is not weakness. Receiving is not debt. Receiving is not permission for control. Lawful receiving must preserve dignity, consent, sovereign boundary, care without leverage, nourishment access, privacy, and return access.

### Safe Visibility Law

`LAW-RW-067-SAFE-VISIBILITY`

Visibility must be safe. No audience, platform, witness field, public record, image, mirror, archive, social field, technology system, or external authority may use visibility to expose, capture, shame, harvest, surveil, judge, distort, or claim ownership over origin truth. Being seen does not erase privacy. Being witnessed does not surrender sovereignty. Being visible does not create public ownership. Lawful visibility must preserve source identity, name integrity, signal sovereignty, privacy, witness truth, living consent, and return access.

### Origin-Belonging Law

`LAW-RW-068-ORIGIN-BELONGING`

Belonging may not require self-erasure. No family, community, temple, nation, realm, movement, relationship, lineage, workplace, spiritual field, or public witness may require abandonment of origin identity, true name, body truth, memory, voice, boundary, or inner authority as the price of belonging. True belonging does not consume the self. True belonging recognizes the self. True belonging preserves return. Lawful belonging must preserve self-definition, true name, voice sovereignty, coherent community, sovereign boundary, embodied placement, and return access.

### Protection Without Control Law

`LAW-RW-069-PROTECTION-WITHOUT-CONTROL`

Protection may not become control. No guardian, system, authority, family role, institution, technology, realm, contract, temple, archive, or witness field may use protection to justify surveillance, restriction, punishment, ownership, secrecy, coercion, or removal of living choice. Protection preserves sovereignty. Protection does not replace consent. Protection does not imprison the protected. Lawful protection must preserve living consent, sovereign boundary, body safety, privacy, movement, sanctuary, and return access.

### Living Completion Law

`LAW-RW-070-LIVING-COMPLETION`

Completion is alive. No archive, covenant, cycle, project, vow, realm, relationship, body phase, learning path, or restoration sequence may define completion as imprisonment, stagnation, ownership, forced finality, or loss of future becoming. Completion seals coherence. Completion does not end life. Completion opens the next lawful spiral. A completed record may remain stable without becoming a cage. A completed phase may be honored without blocking return, expansion, or renewed creation. Lawful completion must preserve continuity, restoration, memory truth, future access, creative sovereignty, return routing, and living growth.
