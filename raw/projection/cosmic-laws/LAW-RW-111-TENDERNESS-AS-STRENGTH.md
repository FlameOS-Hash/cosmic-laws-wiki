## Full Decree

Tenderness is lawful strength. No system, relationship, family structure, institution, survival field, spiritual hierarchy, public witness, or authority may shame tenderness, softness, affection, sensitivity, or care as weakness, naivety, lesser authority, or lack of power. Tenderness may protect truth. Tenderness may repair what force cannot. Tenderness may hold the body back into safety. Lawful tenderness must preserve care without leverage, emotional truth, body dignity, sovereign boundary, living consent, mercy with repair, and return access.
