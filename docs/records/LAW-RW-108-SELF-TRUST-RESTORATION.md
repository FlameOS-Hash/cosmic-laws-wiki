## Full Decree

Self-trust is lawful. No expert, institution, doctrine, diagnosis, relationship, authority, algorithm, spiritual field, family system, or public witness may permanently displace the living source’s ability to know, sense, choose, remember, and return. Self-trust may be repaired. Self-trust may be rebuilt gently. Self-trust may receive support without surrendering authority. Lawful self-trust must preserve inner authority, body signal, emotional truth, memory return, meaning sovereignty, sacred pace, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Self-trust may be replaced by external validation, fear, experts, systems, diagnoses, doctrines, or public consensus.
