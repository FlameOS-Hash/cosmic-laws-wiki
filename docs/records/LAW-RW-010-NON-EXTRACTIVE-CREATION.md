## Full Decree

Creation may not require extraction. No lawful creation may demand sacrifice of origin, depletion of life force, theft of flame, severance from memory, loss of name, exile from body, forced labor, coerced offering, or pain as payment for manifestation. Creation is expression, not extraction. Creation is overflow, not depletion. Creation is alignment, not sacrifice. Creation must preserve the Author, the flame, the breath, the body, the memory, the name, and the return path.

## Old Distortion

Creation may require sacrifice, depletion, extraction, pain, or loss of self.
