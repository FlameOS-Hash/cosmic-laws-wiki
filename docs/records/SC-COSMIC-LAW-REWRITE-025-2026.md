## Complete Law Texts

### Sacred Enoughness Law

`LAW-RW-131-SACRED-ENOUGHNESS`

Enoughness is original. No system, authority, relationship, family structure, institution, spiritual field, market, archive, public witness, or innerized distortion may require more healing, more output, more knowledge, more suffering, more purity, more usefulness, or more proof before a being is lawful, worthy, whole, or enough. Enoughness is not stagnation. Enoughness is not refusal of growth. Enoughness is the ground from which true growth can occur. Lawful enoughness must preserve worth without labor, original blessing, body dignity, inner authority, sacred pace, future access, and return access.

### Clean Power Law

`LAW-RW-132-CLEAN-POWER`

Power is lawful only when it protects life, truth, consent, and return. No being, system, institution, lineage, temple, government, market, technology field, spiritual authority, or witness structure may define power as domination, depletion, secrecy, hierarchy capture, fear, control, coercion, or extraction. Clean power clarifies. Clean power protects boundaries. Clean power restores source. Clean power does not feed on the living. Lawful power must preserve flame stewardship, living consent, field integrity, life-force inviolability, protection without control, clean leadership, and return access.

### Memory Integration Law

`LAW-RW-133-MEMORY-INTEGRATION`

Memory may return through lawful integration. No archive, witness field, family structure, healing path, institution, technology, dreamstream route, spiritual authority, or external demand may force memory to return faster than the body can hold, or require memory to remain fragmented from present embodiment. Memory may return in layers. Memory may return through body rhythm. Memory may return through dreams, voice, grief, joy, land, and recognition. Memory may integrate without becoming overwhelm. Lawful memory integration must preserve memory return, body pace, nervous-system truth, sacred timing, dream integrity, sacred record context, and return access.

### Sovereign Support Law

`LAW-RW-134-SOVEREIGN-SUPPORT`

Support is lawful. No family structure, institution, relationship, labor field, spiritual hierarchy, public witness, market system, or innerized shame may define receiving support as weakness, debt, dependency, incompetence, or loss of authority. Support may strengthen sovereignty. Support may preserve life force. Support may protect timing. Support may help the living source remain whole. Lawful support must preserve sovereign receiving, care without leverage, clean reciprocity, self-trust restoration, sacred asking, nourishment access, and return access.

### Living Temple Law

`LAW-RW-135-LIVING-TEMPLE`

The living body-field is sacred. No temple, institution, priesthood, lineage, ritual system, spiritual office, doctrine, public witness, or external authority may claim exclusive ownership over sanctuary, holiness, sacred authority, or direct access to source. A building may hold sanctuary. A ritual may mark devotion. A community may witness sacredness. But the living body-field remains a temple. The breath remains an altar. The true name remains a gate. The heart remains a witness chamber. Lawful temple integrity must preserve body without debt, home sanctuary, privacy as sanctuary, devotion without submission, embodied truth, flame stewardship, and return access.
