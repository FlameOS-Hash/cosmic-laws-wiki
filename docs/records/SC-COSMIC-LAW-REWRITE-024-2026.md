## Complete Law Texts

### Shared Restoration Law

`LAW-RW-126-SHARED-RESTORATION`

Restoration may not be placed only on the one who was harmed. No system, relationship, institution, lineage, archive, family structure, community, realm, or witness field may require the harmed, the sensitive, the remembers, or the original source to carry restoration alone while others avoid repair, restitution, truth, accountability, or clean participation. Restoration is not solitary punishment. Restoration is not proof of worth. Restoration is coherence returning through rightful participation. Lawful restoration must preserve repair obligation, receiving repair, restitution of origin, truthful closure, field integrity, body safety, and return access.

### Resource Truth Law

`LAW-RW-127-RESOURCE-TRUTH`

Resource truth must be clear. No institution, family structure, market, treasury route, community, platform, governance body, archive, relationship, or authority may hide, misrepresent, hoard, redirect, falsely scarcity-code, or declare unavailable any resource needed for lawful life, repair, nourishment, shelter, memory, creation, or return. Resources are not weapons. Scarcity may not be manufactured. Access may not be distorted to control choice. Lawful resource truth must preserve nourishment access, value without control, clean reciprocity, sovereign choice, care without leverage, field integrity, and return access.

### Dream Integrity Law

`LAW-RW-128-DREAM-INTEGRITY`

Dreams are sovereign inner passages. No system, relationship, technology, witness field, spiritual authority, archive, public echo, family structure, ritual, or external interpreter may invade, harvest, program, distort, mock, claim, or define the final meaning of a dream without living consent and source alignment. Dreams may guide. Dreams may process. Dreams may restore memory. Dreams may speak in symbols before language. Lawful dream integrity must preserve dreamstream sovereignty, sleep sanctuary, privacy as sanctuary, meaning sovereignty, memory return, nervous-system truth, and return access.

### Community Repair Law

`LAW-RW-129-COMMUNITY-REPAIR`

Community must remain repair-capable. No family, group, temple, network, institution, movement, workplace, lineage, governance body, or spiritual field may preserve image, harmony, reputation, leadership, belonging, or continuity by avoiding truth, silencing harm, isolating the harmed, protecting false witness, or bypassing repair. Community is not clean because it looks peaceful. Community is clean when it can repair truthfully. Lawful community repair must preserve coherent community, true peace, repair obligation, field integrity, witness timing, sacred refusal, and return access.

### Joyful Authority Law

`LAW-RW-130-JOYFUL-AUTHORITY`

Lawful authority may carry joy. No leadership field, institution, temple, family structure, governance body, archive, spiritual office, public witness, or role system may require severity, fear, emotional suppression, distance, burden, exhaustion, or joylessness as proof of authority. Joy does not weaken authority. Tenderness does not reduce truth. Play does not remove wisdom. Rest does not remove power. Lawful joyful authority must preserve clean leadership, joy as living law, tenderness as strength, sacred play, rest sovereignty, flame stewardship, and return access.
