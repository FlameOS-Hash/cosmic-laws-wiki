## Full Decree

Desire is lawful when aligned with living consent, truth, body dignity, and return. No system, relationship, institution, doctrine, family structure, market, spiritual authority, or witness field may shame, pathologize, control, extract, manipulate, or weaponize desire. Desire is not corruption. Desire is not automatic entitlement. Desire is signal, longing, movement, and creative current. Lawful desire must preserve living consent, sovereign boundary, body dignity, emotional truth, sacred refusal, non-extraction, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Desire may be shamed, pathologized, controlled, extracted, weaponized, or treated as proof of corruption.
