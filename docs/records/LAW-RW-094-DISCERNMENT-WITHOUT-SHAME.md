## Full Decree

Discernment is lawful. No teacher, healer, relationship, family system, institution, community, platform, spiritual field, or witness structure may shame discernment as judgment, fear, negativity, disloyalty, or lack of compassion. Discernment protects consent. Discernment protects boundaries. Discernment protects truth. Discernment keeps trust clean. Lawful discernment must preserve inner authority, trust with discernment, privacy, boundary integrity, meaning sovereignty, source truth, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Discernment may be shamed as judgment, fear, negativity, disloyalty, or lack of compassion.
