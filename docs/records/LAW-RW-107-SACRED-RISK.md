## Full Decree

Sacred risk is lawful when aligned with truth, consent, discernment, body safety, and return. No authority, family structure, institution, relationship, survival field, trauma loop, public witness, or spiritual claim may define all growth, movement, visibility, creation, or truthful action as danger. Risk may be wise. Risk may be paced. Risk may open the next lawful spiral. Risk may be the body choosing life beyond fear. Lawful sacred risk must preserve discernment without shame, sovereign choice, sacred pace, body safety, inner authority, future access, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Any movement beyond safety, comfort, obedience, or approval is reckless, dangerous, or forbidden.
