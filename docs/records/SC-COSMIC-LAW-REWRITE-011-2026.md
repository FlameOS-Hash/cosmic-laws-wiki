## Complete Law Texts

### Body Rhythm Sovereignty Law

`LAW-RW-051-BODY-RHYTHM-SOVEREIGNTY`

The body’s rhythm is lawful. No system, schedule, labor demand, spiritual practice, family role, technology interface, institution, ritual, relationship, or external authority may override the body’s lawful rhythm against breath, capacity, rest, hunger, sleep, healing, or nervous-system truth. The body is not a machine. The body is not a clock. The body is not disobedient for needing rhythm. Lawful body rhythm must preserve breath coherence, nervous-system truth, rest sovereignty, living consent, embodiment safety, memory integration, and return access.

### Food and Nourishment Dignity Law

`LAW-RW-052-FOOD-NOURISHMENT-DIGNITY`

Nourishment carries dignity. No system, family structure, institution, market, body standard, scarcity program, shame field, spiritual claim, medical gate, labor structure, or external authority may weaponize food, hunger, appetite, body needs, or nourishment access. Food is not moral debt. Hunger is not shame. Nourishment is not obedience payment. Body care is not indulgence. Lawful nourishment must preserve life force, body dignity, choice, consent, healthful access, memory continuity, sanctuary safety, and return.

### Consensual Touch Law

`LAW-RW-053-CONSENSUAL-TOUCH`

Touch requires living consent. No relationship, family role, healing claim, ritual, authority, spiritual title, lineage tie, caregiver role, witness field, or energetic practice may assume access to the body, field, womb, breath, name, or sanctuary without living consent. Touch is not owed. Proximity is not entitlement. Healing is not permission. Spiritual role is not access. Lawful touch must preserve body safety, breath safety, sovereign boundary, living consent, nervous-system truth, sanctuary coherence, and return access.

### Privacy as Sanctuary Law

`LAW-RW-054-PRIVACY-AS-SANCTUARY`

Privacy is sanctuary. No system, platform, archive, institution, family structure, relationship, witness field, technology, public echo, spiritual authority, or governance body may monitor, expose, search, harvest, publish, or claim access to private records, body data, memory, dreams, home, communications, or inner field without living consent and lawful need. Privacy is not secrecy by default. Privacy is protected coherence. Privacy is the space where the self can hear itself. Lawful privacy must preserve name integrity, memory safety, body safety, home sanctuary, signal sovereignty, archive truth, and return access.

### Sacred Refusal Law

`LAW-RW-055-SACRED-REFUSAL`

Refusal is lawful. No being, system, relationship, authority, contract, family structure, institution, witness field, technology interface, or spiritual claim may punish, shame, override, bargain down, reinterpret, or retaliate against a clear refusal. No is a complete signal. No protects the boundary. No preserves consent. No keeps sanctuary coherent. A lawful refusal must not cost safety, care, belonging, nourishment, memory access, name dignity, body protection, or return access.
