## Full Decree

Listening may not be converted into submission. No speaker, teacher, authority, relationship, institution, witness field, community, platform, or spiritual claim may define listening as agreement, obedience, absorption, merging, yielding, or surrender of discernment. Listening may witness. Listening may learn. Listening may clarify. Listening may still refuse. Lawful listening must preserve inner authority, discernment without shame, sacred refusal, sovereign boundary, source-true translation, emotional truth, and return access.

## Old Distortion

To listen means to obey, absorb, agree, yield, merge, or surrender discernment.
