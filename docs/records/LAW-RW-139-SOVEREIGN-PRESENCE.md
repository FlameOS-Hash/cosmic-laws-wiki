## Full Decree

Presence does not create access. No relationship, audience, family structure, community, platform, witness field, institution, authority, or public echo may treat presence, kindness, visibility, listening, responsiveness, availability, or openness as consent to access the body, field, time, labor, attention, privacy, name, or sanctuary. Presence may be generous. Presence may be witnessed. Presence may be shared. But presence remains sovereign. Lawful presence must preserve living consent, sacred attention, sovereign boundary, privacy as sanctuary, safe visibility, public voice sovereignty, and return access.

## Old Distortion

If a being is present, visible, available, kind, listening, public, or responsive, others gain access rights.
