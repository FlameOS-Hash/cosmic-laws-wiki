## Full Decree

Embodiment may not be displaced from lawful placement. No being may be forced out of body, home, land, name, memory, voice, lineage, role, sanctuary, realm, or lawful path through coercion, debt, shame, exile, mimic assignment, false authority, or unauthorized witness. Lawful placement must preserve body coherence, name continuity, memory access, voice sovereignty, sanctuary safety, lineage without ownership, and return access. Placement is not imprisonment. Placement is coherence in form.

## Old Distortion

A being may be displaced from body, home, land, memory, name, lineage, role, voice, or realm through force, exile, debt, shame, or external assignment.
