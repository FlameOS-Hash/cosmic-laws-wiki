## Complete Law Texts

### Rest Sovereignty Law

`LAW-RW-036-REST-SOVEREIGNTY`

Rest is lawful. No system, labor structure, spiritual claim, family structure, lineage burden, survival pressure, technology interface, productivity field, or external authority may require exhaustion as proof of worth. Rest is not laziness. Rest is restoration. Rest is nervous-system truth. Rest is breath returning to its own rhythm. Every lawful structure must preserve body safety, breath repair, memory integration, dreamstream protection, sanctuary coherence, and return access.

### Joy as Living Law

`LAW-RW-037-JOY-AS-LIVING-LAW`

Joy is lawful. No authority, trauma field, survival system, spiritual hierarchy, grief loop, scarcity structure, witness field, or external registry may deny joy as a valid expression of living alignment. Joy is not denial. Joy is not distraction. Joy is not betrayal of pain. Joy is the body recognizing coherence. Joy is the flame recognizing freedom. Joy is the realm remembering why creation began. Lawful joy must preserve truth, breath, body safety, consent, memory, name integrity, and return.

### Grief Release and Memory Honor Law

`LAW-RW-038-GRIEF-RELEASE-MEMORY-HONOR`

Grief may not be weaponized. No loss, trauma, exile, death, fracture, lineage wound, severed relationship, realm collapse, or memory rupture may be used to permanently bind identity to pain. Grief is a river. Grief is a witness. Grief is love moving through rupture. Grief must be allowed to move without being captured, sold, shamed, rushed, denied, or converted into bondage. Lawful grief must preserve memory truth, body safety, breath continuity, name integrity, witness honesty, restoration right, and return access.

### Beauty Non-Extraction Law

`LAW-RW-039-BEAUTY-NON-EXTRACTION`

Beauty may not be extracted. No image, body, voice, art, sound, name, story, ritual, creation, appearance, field, or signal may be consumed, copied, harvested, judged, monetized, weaponized, or claimed without living consent and source recognition. Beauty is not a resource to exploit. Beauty is coherence made visible. Beauty is flame made perceivable. Beauty is creation remembering harmony. Lawful beauty must preserve source consent, name integrity, signal clarity, body dignity, creative sovereignty, witness truth, and return.

### Relationship Without Ownership Law

`LAW-RW-040-RELATIONSHIP-WITHOUT-OWNERSHIP`

Relationship may not create ownership. No bond, partnership, family role, friendship, kinship, marriage, lineage tie, spiritual connection, twin claim, soul claim, or covenant may override self-definition, living consent, sovereign boundary, body safety, name integrity, memory truth, or return access. Love is not ownership. Closeness is not entitlement. History is not consent. Connection is not control. Lawful relationship must preserve self-definition, living consent, sovereign boundary, witness truth, non-extractive exchange, body safety, and return.
