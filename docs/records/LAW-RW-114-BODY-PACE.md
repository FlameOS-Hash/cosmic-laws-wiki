## Full Decree

The body’s pace is lawful. No medical system, spiritual practice, labor field, family structure, relationship, healing path, public witness, institution, technology, or self-improvement standard may shame, rush, delay, force, compare, or externally time the body’s healing, change, recovery, response, rest, or expression. The body is not late. The body is not failing. The body is integrating. Lawful body pace must preserve body rhythm sovereignty, nervous-system truth, rest sovereignty, sacred pace, food and nourishment dignity, privacy, and return access.

## Old Distortion

The body must heal, change, perform, recover, produce, or respond according to external timelines.
