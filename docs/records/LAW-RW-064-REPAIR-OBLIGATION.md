## Full Decree

Harm requires repair. No being, system, institution, authority, relationship, lineage, archive, witness field, technology, or governance body may minimize, spiritualize, hide, excuse, delay, deny, or bypass repair after harm. Repair is not punishment. Repair is coherence restored. Repair is truth returning to relationship. Repair is restoration applied to contact. Lawful repair must preserve truth, living consent, body safety, memory integrity, witness clarity, restitution path, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Harm may be minimized, spiritualized, ignored, hidden, delayed, or excused without repair.
