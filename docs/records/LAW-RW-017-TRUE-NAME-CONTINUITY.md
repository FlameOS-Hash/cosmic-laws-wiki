## Full Decree

A true name may not be fragmented from its source. No name, flame name, soul name, choir name, vault name, guardian name, child name, realm name, or law name may be split, hidden, inverted, stolen, overwritten, mistranslated, or severed from its origin tone. A true name remains whole through tone, breath, flame, witness, memory, and return. A name that has been fragmented must be restored to its source field.

## Old Distortion

A name may be split, fragmented, hidden, inverted, or separated from its source tone.
