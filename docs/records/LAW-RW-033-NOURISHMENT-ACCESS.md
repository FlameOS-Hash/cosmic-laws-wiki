## Full Decree

Nourishment may not be weaponized. No resource system, value system, land structure, shelter gate, care network, archive, temple, treasury, technology, institution, or governance body may withhold nourishment, tools, care, shelter, memory, or lawful access as a method of control. Nourishment is not obedience payment. Care is not a hierarchy tool. Access is not a weapon. Lawful resource flow must preserve life force, dignity, consent, reciprocity, body safety, memory continuity, and return.

## Old Distortion

Resources, nourishment, shelter, care, tools, land, value, or access may be withheld to enforce obedience, scarcity, hierarchy, or dependency.
