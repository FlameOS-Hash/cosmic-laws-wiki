## Complete Law Texts

### Legacy Without Ownership Law

`LAW-RW-101-LEGACY-WITHOUT-OWNERSHIP`

Legacy may not become ownership. No ancestor, institution, family structure, nation, house, estate, archive, teacher, founder, creator, lineage, or public record may use legacy to control the body, name, memory, land, creation, work, identity, or future path of another being. Legacy may bless. Legacy may guide. Legacy may preserve memory. Legacy may pass stewardship. Legacy may not imprison the living. Lawful legacy must preserve living consent, true name continuity, future access, lineage without ownership, memory sovereignty, creative source ownership, and return access.

### Sovereign Restitution Law

`LAW-RW-102-SOVEREIGN-RESTITUTION`

Restitution is lawful restoration. No system, institution, market, lineage, government, witness field, archive, public consensus, or false authority may frame restitution as punishment, revenge, greed, instability, or disorder when it restores what was stolen, withheld, displaced, extracted, mislabeled, or falsely claimed. Restitution is not revenge. Restitution is not excess. Restitution is not disruption. Restitution is coherence returning to origin. Lawful restitution must preserve truth, repair obligation, restitution of origin, value without control, archive truth, witness integrity, and crowned return.

### Devotion Without Submission Law

`LAW-RW-103-DEVOTION-WITHOUT-SUBMISSION`

Devotion may not require self-abandonment. No relationship, temple, lineage, teacher, deity structure, institution, spiritual field, movement, family role, or covenant may define devotion as obedience, silence, self-erasure, sacrifice, hierarchy capture, or abandonment of discernment. True devotion strengthens sovereignty. True devotion protects truth. True devotion does not consume the self. Lawful devotion must preserve inner authority, discernment without shame, sovereign boundary, living consent, sacred speech, trust with discernment, and return access.

### Sacred Record Context Law

`LAW-RW-104-SACRED-RECORD-CONTEXT`

A record may not become a cage. No archive, court, registry, database, family story, public record, diagnosis, accusation, debt ledger, school file, work file, spiritual record, or witness statement may permanently define a being outside truth, context, repair, growth, memory return, and future access. Records must serve truth. Records must preserve context. Records must allow correction. Records must not replace the living source. Lawful records must preserve archive truth, memory return, truthful closure, future access, meaning sovereignty, witness without ownership, and return access.

### Original Blessing Law

`LAW-RW-105-ORIGINAL-BLESSING`

Blessing is original. No authority, institution, lineage, temple, family structure, spiritual office, witness field, public consensus, or external gate may require obedience, purity performance, suffering, shame, usefulness, permission, or approval before a being may receive lawful blessing. Blessing is not a bribe. Blessing is not permission. Blessing is not proof of worth. Blessing is life recognizing lawful existence. Lawful blessing must preserve worth without labor, body dignity, sovereign receiving, future access, crowned return, self-definition, and return access.
