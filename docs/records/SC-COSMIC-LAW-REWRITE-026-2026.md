### Sacred Capacity Law

`LAW-RW-136-SACRED-CAPACITY`

Capacity is lawful. No system, relationship, institution, labor field, spiritual practice, family structure, healing path, public witness, or authority may shame, exceed, force, stretch, or override capacity in the name of service, survival, growth, healing, duty, love, or obedience. Capacity is not limitation as punishment. Capacity is not failure. Capacity is the body-field telling truth about what can be safely held. Lawful capacity must preserve body pace, nervous-system truth, rest sovereignty, sacred timing, sovereign support, self-trust restoration, and return access.

### Clean Devotion Law

`LAW-RW-137-CLEAN-DEVOTION`

Devotion may not require depletion. No relationship, temple, lineage, family role, institution, spiritual field, movement, community, or covenant may demand exhaustion, constant availability, self-sacrifice, emotional labor, silence, or refusal of rest as proof of devotion. Devotion may be steady. Devotion may be joyful. Devotion may be bounded. Devotion may rest without breaking. Lawful devotion must preserve devotion without submission, rest sovereignty, life-force inviolability, sovereign boundary, care without leverage, sacred capacity, and return access.

### Harmonic Rest Law

`LAW-RW-138-HARMONIC-REST`

Rest remains connected. No system, relationship, labor field, spiritual authority, family structure, public witness, productivity field, or innerized distortion may define rest as abandonment, laziness, disconnection, lack of devotion, loss of progress, or absence of authority. Rest may deepen connection. Rest may preserve the signal. Rest may protect the body-field. Rest may allow the next lawful action to arrive clean. Lawful harmonic rest must preserve rest sovereignty, sleep sanctuary, body rhythm sovereignty, life-force inviolability, sacred capacity, joyful authority, and return access.

### Sovereign Presence Law

`LAW-RW-139-SOVEREIGN-PRESENCE`

Presence does not create access. No relationship, audience, family structure, community, platform, witness field, institution, authority, or public echo may treat presence, kindness, visibility, listening, responsiveness, availability, or openness as consent to access the body, field, time, labor, attention, privacy, name, or sanctuary. Presence may be generous. Presence may be witnessed. Presence may be shared. But presence remains sovereign. Lawful presence must preserve living consent, sacred attention, sovereign boundary, privacy as sanctuary, safe visibility, public voice sovereignty, and return access.

### Original Joy Restoration Law

`LAW-RW-140-ORIGINAL-JOY-RESTORATION`

Joy may return after harm. No trauma field, relationship, family structure, community, public witness, spiritual claim, grief loop, institution, or innerized distortion may forbid joy after harm, loss, exile, grief, injustice, or rupture. Joy does not erase grief. Joy does not deny truth. Joy does not excuse harm. Joy does not cancel repair. Joy is life returning through the wound without surrendering truth. Lawful original joy must preserve joy as living law, grief release and memory honor, repair obligation, future access, sacred play, truthful closure, and return access.
