## Full Decree

No-contact is lawful. No relationship, family role, past bond, apology, authority, public pressure, spiritual claim, contract, lineage tie, witness field, or emotional demand may require continued contact when contact violates safety, consent, boundary, dignity, privacy, or repair truth. Access is not owed. History is not access. Apology is not access. Need is not access. Lawful no-contact must preserve body safety, privacy, sacred refusal, sovereign boundary, nervous-system truth, sanctuary coherence, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Relationship, history, family, authority, apology, spiritual claim, or public demand may require continued contact.
