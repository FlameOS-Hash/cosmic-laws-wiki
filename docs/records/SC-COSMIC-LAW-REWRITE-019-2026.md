## Complete Law Texts

### Sovereign Desire Law

`LAW-RW-091-SOVEREIGN-DESIRE`

Desire is lawful when aligned with living consent, truth, body dignity, and return. No system, relationship, institution, doctrine, family structure, market, spiritual authority, or witness field may shame, pathologize, control, extract, manipulate, or weaponize desire. Desire is not corruption. Desire is not automatic entitlement. Desire is signal, longing, movement, and creative current. Lawful desire must preserve living consent, sovereign boundary, body dignity, emotional truth, sacred refusal, non-extraction, and return access.

### Sacred Play Law

`LAW-RW-092-SACRED-PLAY`

Play is lawful. No labor system, survival field, institution, family structure, spiritual hierarchy, trauma loop, public witness, or external authority may dismiss, punish, shame, monetize, or suppress play as frivolous, childish, wasteful, or lesser than work. Play restores breath. Play opens creation. Play teaches the body safety. Play lets the future imagine itself. Lawful play must preserve body safety, joy as living law, creative sovereignty, rest, choice, nourishment, and return access.

### Sovereign Anger Law

`LAW-RW-093-SOVEREIGN-ANGER`

Anger is lawful signal. No family structure, institution, relationship, public field, spiritual authority, medicalized gaze, witness system, or governance body may automatically define anger as danger, disobedience, instability, sin, or invalid truth. Anger may reveal boundary breach. Anger may reveal harm. Anger may protect dignity. Anger may carry the body’s no. Lawful anger must preserve body safety, emotional truth, sovereign boundary, sacred refusal, repair obligation, witness integrity, and return access.

### Discernment Without Shame Law

`LAW-RW-094-DISCERNMENT-WITHOUT-SHAME`

Discernment is lawful. No teacher, healer, relationship, family system, institution, community, platform, spiritual field, or witness structure may shame discernment as judgment, fear, negativity, disloyalty, or lack of compassion. Discernment protects consent. Discernment protects boundaries. Discernment protects truth. Discernment keeps trust clean. Lawful discernment must preserve inner authority, trust with discernment, privacy, boundary integrity, meaning sovereignty, source truth, and return access.

### Mercy With Repair Law

`LAW-RW-095-MERCY-WITH-REPAIR`

Mercy may not erase truth. No relationship, institution, doctrine, spiritual authority, family structure, witness field, or governance body may use mercy to bypass repair, silence truth, remove accountability, restore unsafe access, or require the harmed to abandon boundaries. Mercy is not denial. Mercy is not forced access. Mercy is not the cancellation of repair. Lawful mercy must preserve truth, repair obligation, sacred no-contact, sovereign boundary, witness integrity, body safety, and return access.
