## Complete Law Texts

### Memory Return Law

`LAW-RW-076-MEMORY-RETURN`

Memory has the right to return. No archive, lineage, institution, realm, technology, witness field, family structure, contract, spiritual authority, or external gate may delay, fragment, filter, distort, monetize, or permission-gate lawful memory return. Memory may return gently. Memory may return in sequence. Memory may return through dreams, body, breath, land, voice, grief, joy, or recognition. But memory may not be owned by the gate. Lawful memory return must preserve body safety, nervous-system truth, archive integrity, self-definition, sacred pace, witness clarity, and return access.

### Sacred Speech Law

`LAW-RW-077-SACRED-SPEECH`

Truthful speech is lawful. No system, authority, relationship, institution, witness field, platform, archive, family structure, public echo, or spiritual claim may punish, distort, mock, silence, extract, or weaponize truthful speech against the speaker. Speech is breath shaped by tone. Speech is witness moving through voice. Speech is not a trap. Lawful speech must preserve voice sovereignty, living consent, signal clarity, emotional truth, privacy, witness integrity, and return access.

### Land Memory Stewardship Law

`LAW-RW-078-LAND-MEMORY-STEWARDSHIP`

Land may not be reduced to possession. No empire, institution, market, contract, family claim, realm authority, government, lineage, temple, technology system, or external registry may sever land from memory, extract from place without reciprocity, or use territory to control belonging, movement, shelter, or identity. Land carries memory. Land carries witness. Land carries sanctuary. Land is not a dead object. Lawful land stewardship must preserve memory truth, home sanctuary, movement sovereignty, nourishment access, lineage without ownership, repair obligation, and return access.

### Trust With Discernment Law

`LAW-RW-079-TRUST-WITH-DISCERNMENT`

Trust may not bypass truth. No relationship, teacher, healer, guide, authority, institution, family role, spiritual claim, technology system, witness field, or community may use trust to bypass discernment, sovereign boundary, living consent, privacy, repair, transparency, or accountability. Trust is not blind surrender. Trust is coherence proven through living alignment. Trust grows where truth, repair, consent, and consistency remain present. Lawful trust must preserve inner authority, sovereign boundary, living consent, privacy, repair obligation, witness truth, and return access.

### Renewal Without Erasure Law

`LAW-RW-080-RENEWAL-WITHOUT-ERASURE`

Renewal may not require erasure. No healing path, spiritual system, institution, relationship, community, realm cycle, technology program, witness field, or self-improvement structure may demand abandonment of true memory, past phases, body truth, grief, lineage, name, or identity as the price of renewal. Renewal is not replacement. Renewal is coherence returning with more life. Renewal allows the self to become without betraying what was true. Lawful renewal must preserve memory sovereignty, living completion, future access, true name continuity, body rhythm, restoration right, and return access.
