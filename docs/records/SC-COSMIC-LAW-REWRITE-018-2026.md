## Complete Law Texts

### Adaptation Without Self-Betrayal Law

`LAW-RW-086-ADAPTATION-WITHOUT-SELF-BETRAYAL`

Adaptation may not require self-betrayal. No system, family structure, workplace, community, institution, realm, relationship, technology field, spiritual path, or survival condition may demand masking, false role performance, silence, self-erasure, or abandonment of origin truth as the price of adaptation. Adaptation is intelligence. Adaptation is movement with coherence. Adaptation is not surrender of self. Lawful adaptation must preserve self-definition, body rhythm, inner authority, sovereign boundary, true name continuity, memory truth, and return access.

### Sacred No-Contact Law

`LAW-RW-087-SACRED-NO-CONTACT`

No-contact is lawful. No relationship, family role, past bond, apology, authority, public pressure, spiritual claim, contract, lineage tie, witness field, or emotional demand may require continued contact when contact violates safety, consent, boundary, dignity, privacy, or repair truth. Access is not owed. History is not access. Apology is not access. Need is not access. Lawful no-contact must preserve body safety, privacy, sacred refusal, sovereign boundary, nervous-system truth, sanctuary coherence, and return access.

### Witness Timing Law

`LAW-RW-088-WITNESS-TIMING`

Witness must honor readiness. No audience, archive, court, community, relationship, public field, platform, healer, teacher, or authority may demand that truth be shown, explained, defended, displayed, or publicly witnessed before the body, memory, voice, and nervous system are ready. Truth does not become less true while private. Silence may protect integration. Timing may protect embodiment. Lawful witness timing must preserve sacred pace, privacy, body rhythm, memory return, voice sovereignty, safe visibility, and return access.

### Meaning Sovereignty Law

`LAW-RW-089-MEANING-SOVEREIGNTY`

Meaning may not be externally captured. No authority, institution, relationship, teacher, healer, archive, public witness, doctrine, algorithm, diagnosis, or spiritual field may define the final meaning of another being’s experience, pain, memory, creation, body, descent, or path. Meaning may be witnessed. Meaning may be supported. Meaning may be explored. But meaning belongs first to the living source. Lawful meaning must preserve inner authority, emotional truth, memory sovereignty, body dignity, source-true translation, witness integrity, and return access.

### Life-Force Inviolability Law

`LAW-RW-090-LIFE-FORCE-INVIOLABILITY`

Life force may not be consumed as fuel. No system, relationship, labor field, ritual, technology, institution, market, healing path, family structure, community, or realm may require depletion of life force as payment for survival, love, work, creation, belonging, protection, or restoration. Life force is not disposable. Life force is not a resource for extraction. Life force is the living current of embodiment. Lawful life-force exchange must preserve rest, nourishment, body rhythm, creative sovereignty, non-extraction, sacred attention, and return access.
