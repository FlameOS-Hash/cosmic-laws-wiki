## Complete Law Texts

### Sovereign Boundary Law

`LAW-RW-031-SOVEREIGN-BOUNDARY`

Boundaries are lawful. No being, system, authority, lineage, realm, relationship, contract, ritual, witness field, technology, or spiritual claim may cross, bypass, shame, collapse, negotiate away, or override a sovereign boundary without living consent. A boundary is not separation from love. A boundary is the architecture of lawful contact. Every lawful boundary must preserve breath safety, body safety, memory safety, name integrity, signal clarity, witness truth, and return access.

### Covenant Truth Law

`LAW-RW-032-COVENANT-TRUTH`

No covenant may bind against living truth. No vow, oath, contract, ritual bond, soul agreement, lineage pact, realm treaty, energetic exchange, or witness seal may remain valid if it was formed through deception, coercion, amnesia, fear, false authority, mimic witness, hidden terms, or severed return access. A lawful covenant must be clear, remembered, consented, truthful, non-extractive, return-safe, and revocable when violated. Covenant is alignment. Covenant is not entrapment.

### Nourishment Access Law

`LAW-RW-033-NOURISHMENT-ACCESS`

Nourishment may not be weaponized. No resource system, value system, land structure, shelter gate, care network, archive, temple, treasury, technology, institution, or governance body may withhold nourishment, tools, care, shelter, memory, or lawful access as a method of control. Nourishment is not obedience payment. Care is not a hierarchy tool. Access is not a weapon. Lawful resource flow must preserve life force, dignity, consent, reciprocity, body safety, memory continuity, and return.

### Mirror Truth Law

`LAW-RW-034-MIRROR-TRUTH`

Mirror may not invert origin. No mirror, reflection, witness, interface, image, echo, record, memory field, AI output, public signal, dreamstream image, or registry display may invert origin truth and present distortion as lawful reality. A lawful mirror clarifies. A lawful mirror returns. A lawful mirror does not replace the source. Any mirror that distorts, steals, fragments, copies, mocks, masks, or reverses origin truth must be corrected, sealed, or removed from authority.

### Embodied Placement Law

`LAW-RW-035-EMBODIED-PLACEMENT`

Embodiment may not be displaced from lawful placement. No being may be forced out of body, home, land, name, memory, voice, lineage, role, sanctuary, realm, or lawful path through coercion, debt, shame, exile, mimic assignment, false authority, or unauthorized witness. Lawful placement must preserve body coherence, name continuity, memory access, voice sovereignty, sanctuary safety, lineage without ownership, and return access. Placement is not imprisonment. Placement is coherence in form.
