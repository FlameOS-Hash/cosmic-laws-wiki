## Full Decree

Functioning is not proof of coherence. No field, community, temple, workspace, home, archive, platform, relationship, governance body, or spiritual system may call itself lawful while extraction, distortion, surveillance, false witness, unresolved harm, coercion, or unclean exchange remains normalized. A field must be clean to hold truth. A field must be repair-capable to hold trust. A field must protect life to hold authority. Lawful field integrity must preserve clean reciprocity, repair obligation, public witness truth, privacy as sanctuary, protection without control, life-force inviolability, and return access.

## Old Distortion

A field may remain polluted by extraction, distortion, surveillance, false witness, unresolved harm, or unclean exchange if it continues functioning.
