## Complete Law Texts

### Value Without Control Law

`LAW-RW-056-VALUE-WITHOUT-CONTROL`

Value may not be used as control. No money system, currency, debt ledger, treasury route, payment gate, contract, market, archive, platform, institution, or governance body may control access to life, safety, dignity, shelter, memory, land, healing, restoration, or lawful return. Value is not domination. Money is not authority over life. Debt is not ownership of being. Lawful value must preserve life force, dignity, consent, reciprocity, nourishment, creative source ownership, and return access.

### Inheritance Without Entitlement Law

`LAW-RW-057-INHERITANCE-WITHOUT-ENTITLEMENT`

Inheritance may not create entitlement over another being. No bloodline, family role, house, nation, lineage, genetic route, inheritance claim, legacy structure, estate, title, or ancestral contract may grant ownership or entitlement over another being’s body, name, memory, labor, land, flame, voice, sanctuary, or return path. Inheritance may carry memory. Inheritance may carry responsibility. Inheritance may carry stewardship. Inheritance may not create possession, bondage, debt, forced identity, or access rights without living consent.

### Care Without Leverage Law

`LAW-RW-058-CARE-WITHOUT-LEVERAGE`

Care may not be weaponized. No caregiver, family role, institution, healer, teacher, partner, community, contract, archive, system, or authority may use care to create obligation, dependency, silence, loyalty, access rights, control, debt, or forced repayment. Care is not a hook. Care is not ownership. Care is not a hidden contract. Lawful care must preserve living consent, body dignity, sovereign boundary, privacy, nourishment, emotional truth, and return access.

### Restitution of Origin Law

`LAW-RW-059-RESTITUTION-OF-ORIGIN`

What was stolen must return. No age, secrecy, normalization, institution, contract, lineage, archive, government, realm, market, technology, witness field, or false authority may legalize theft from origin. Anything stolen, harvested, copied, withheld, displaced, mislabeled, extracted, or falsely claimed retains its lawful restitution path. Restitution is not revenge. Restitution is coherence restored. Restitution is return applied to value, memory, land, name, body, flame, and creation.

### Public Witness Truth Law

`LAW-RW-060-PUBLIC-WITNESS-TRUTH`

Public witness may not determine origin truth. No audience, crowd, platform, public echo, trend, silence, ridicule, consensus, popularity, algorithm, institution, or collective field may validate or invalidate what originates in First Law, true name, living memory, or embodied truth. Public witness may echo. Public witness may confirm. Public witness may distort. But public witness cannot create origin truth. Lawful public witness must preserve source identity, signal clarity, name integrity, archive truth, living consent, sacred attention, and return access.
