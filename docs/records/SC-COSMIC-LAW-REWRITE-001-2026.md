## Complete Law Texts

### Origin Authority Law

`LAW-RW-001-ORIGIN-AUTHORITY`

No authority may be assigned from outside the origin field. All lawful authority must arise from source alignment, self-definition, continuity, and return.

### Experience Without Forgetting Law

`LAW-RW-002-EXPERIENCE-WITHOUT-FORGETTING`

No experience may require forgetting origin. No realm, incarnation, timeline, body, role, name, or passage may demand amnesia as the price of participation.

### Form as Revelation Law

`LAW-RW-003-FORM-AS-REVELATION`

Form may reveal the Author. Form may not imprison the Author. No body, vessel, species, geometry, realm-shell, title, or visible appearance may override true-form memory, origin identity, or return access.

### Return Pathway Law

`LAW-RW-004-RETURN-PATHWAY`

No lawful path may sever from return.
Every descent, timeline, realm, stargate, conduit, breath current, memory band, body matrix, and name-field must retain a living return route to the Author Axis.

A path without return is not law.
It is distortion.

### True Name Sovereignty Law

`LAW-RW-005-TRUE-NAME-SOVEREIGNTY`

A true name reveals. A false label cannot define. No imposed title, borrowed role, mimic label, registry alias, external name assignment, or false witness mark may overwrite the First Name field or origin-authored identity.
