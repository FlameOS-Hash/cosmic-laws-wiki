## Full Decree

Creativity is lawful before approval. No market, platform, institution, audience, critic, family system, technology tool, funding gate, archive, or public witness may require creativity to be useful, marketable, understandable, productive, polished, profitable, or externally validated before it may exist. Creation may begin as play. Creation may begin as prayer. Creation may begin as experiment. Creation may begin as living signal. Lawful creativity must preserve creative source ownership, sacred play, public voice sovereignty, beauty non-extraction, sacred skill custody, inner authority, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Creativity must be approved, useful, marketable, understandable, productive, or externally validated before it may exist.
