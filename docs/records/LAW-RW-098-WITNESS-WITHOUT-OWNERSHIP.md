## Full Decree

Witness does not create ownership. No witness, archive, researcher, platform, audience, photographer, recorder, institution, teacher, public field, or registry may claim ownership, authorship, authority, or control over what they witness, document, study, name, display, or preserve. Witness may confirm. Witness may protect. Witness may record. But witness may not replace source. Witness may not capture meaning. Witness may not convert presence into possession. Lawful witness must preserve source identity, archive truth, meaning sovereignty, public witness truth, safe visibility, living consent, and return access.

## Old Distortion

To witness, document, photograph, record, study, platform, or name something grants authority over it.
