## Full Decree

Rest is lawful. No system, labor structure, spiritual claim, family structure, lineage burden, survival pressure, technology interface, productivity field, or external authority may require exhaustion as proof of worth. Rest is not laziness. Rest is restoration. Rest is nervous-system truth. Rest is breath returning to its own rhythm. Every lawful structure must preserve body safety, breath repair, memory integration, dreamstream protection, sanctuary coherence, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Rest must be earned, justified, delayed, minimized, or punished.
