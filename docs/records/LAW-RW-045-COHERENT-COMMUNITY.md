## Full Decree

Community may not require self-erasure. No group, family, tribe, nation, council, temple, network, movement, institution, realm, or spiritual field may require conformity, silence, identity suppression, false role performance, obedience, self-abandonment, or boundary collapse as the price of belonging. True community does not consume identity. True community protects coherence. True community strengthens return. Lawful community must preserve self-definition, true name, living consent, sovereign boundary, voice, nourishment, witness integrity, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Belonging may require conformity, self-erasure, silence, role performance, identity suppression, or group obedience.
