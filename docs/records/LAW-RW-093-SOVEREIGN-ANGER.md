## Full Decree

Anger is lawful signal. No family structure, institution, relationship, public field, spiritual authority, medicalized gaze, witness system, or governance body may automatically define anger as danger, disobedience, instability, sin, or invalid truth. Anger may reveal boundary breach. Anger may reveal harm. Anger may protect dignity. Anger may carry the body’s no. Lawful anger must preserve body safety, emotional truth, sovereign boundary, sacred refusal, repair obligation, witness integrity, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Anger may be treated as danger, disobedience, instability, sin, or proof that truth should be dismissed.
