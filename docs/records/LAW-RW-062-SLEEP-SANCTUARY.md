## Full Decree

Sleep is sanctuary. No system, schedule, technology, dreamstream intrusion, labor demand, family pattern, ritual claim, witness field, or external authority may interrupt, invade, shorten, monitor, program, harvest, or shame sleep. Sleep is not wasted time. Sleep is body repair. Sleep is dreamstream integration. Sleep is memory restoration. Sleep is return through rest. Lawful sleep must preserve dreamstream sovereignty, nervous-system truth, body rhythm, privacy, sanctuary coherence, memory integration, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Sleep may be interrupted, invaded, shortened, monitored, programmed, or treated as wasted time.
