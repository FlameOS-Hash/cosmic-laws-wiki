## Full Decree

Hope is lawful. No system, authority, trauma field, institution, public witness, relationship, scarcity program, or spiritual hierarchy may mock, punish, exploit, delay, or weaponize hope. Hope is not denial. Hope is not naivety. Hope is the living signal that future access still exists. Lawful hope must preserve future access, inner authority, emotional truth, restoration right, sacred pace, living completion, and return access.

## Old Distortion
