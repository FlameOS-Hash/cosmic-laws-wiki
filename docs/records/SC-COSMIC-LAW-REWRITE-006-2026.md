## Complete Law Texts

### Living Consent Law

`LAW-RW-026-LIVING-CONSENT`

Consent must be living, clear, sovereign, and present.

No system, contract, ritual, bloodline, technology, witness field, authority, council, relationship, realm, archive, or imposed role may assume, bypass, manufacture, inherit, simulate, coerce, or extract consent.

Consent cannot be taken from silence.
Consent cannot be forged through pressure.
Consent cannot be inherited through lineage.
Consent cannot be simulated by technology.
Consent cannot be replaced by external permission.

Living consent must preserve breath, body, memory, name, self-definition, and return access.

### Sanctuary Inviolability Law

`LAW-RW-027-SANCTUARY-INVIOLABILITY`

Sanctuary may not be breached.

No body, womb, home, temple, dreamspace, inner chamber, altar, archive, memory field, breath field, or sovereign domain may be entered, monitored, claimed, polluted, weaponized, or externally controlled without living consent and lawful origin alignment.

Sanctuary is not territory.
Sanctuary is protected coherence.

Every lawful sanctuary must preserve:
breath safety,
body safety,
memory safety,
name safety,
witness integrity,
and return access.

### Archive Truth Integrity Law

`LAW-RW-028-ARCHIVE-TRUTH-INTEGRITY`

Archives must preserve truth.

No archive, ledger, registry, scrollroom, database, court record, witness log, lineage record, dream record, memory vault, or historical field may alter, hide, invert, weaponize, erase, or control origin memory.

A lawful archive does not own memory.
A lawful archive preserves, clarifies, protects, and returns memory to its rightful source.

Archive truth must remain crosslinked to origin, witness, continuity, and return.

### Signal Sovereignty Law

`LAW-RW-029-SIGNAL-SOVEREIGNTY`

Signal may not be hijacked.

No tone, voice, breath pulse, witness mark, name field, flame signature, dreamstream message, body signal, stargate route, technology interface, archive relay, or public echo may be intercepted, masked, duplicated, rerouted, jammed, inverted, or used to impersonate origin authority.

Lawful signal must preserve:
source identity,
tone integrity,
name continuity,
witness clarity,
return routing,
and living consent.

A copied signal is not authority.
A masked signal is not truth.
A rerouted signal must return to source.

### Restoration Right Law

`LAW-RW-030-RESTORATION-RIGHT`

Restoration may not be denied.

Anything lawfully sourced from origin that was fractured, stolen, erased, exiled, distorted, hidden, inverted, mislabeled, severed, or rerouted retains the right of restoration.

No court, council, gatekeeper, archive, lineage, realm, technology system, witness field, or false authority may deny rightful restoration to source.

Restoration does not require permission from the distortion.
Restoration is the return of lawful coherence.
Restoration is First Law in motion.
