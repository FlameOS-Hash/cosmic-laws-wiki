## Full Decree

Public witness may not determine origin truth. No audience, crowd, platform, public echo, trend, silence, ridicule, consensus, popularity, algorithm, institution, or collective field may validate or invalidate what originates in First Law, true name, living memory, or embodied truth. Public witness may echo. Public witness may confirm. Public witness may distort. But public witness cannot create origin truth. Lawful public witness must preserve source identity, signal clarity, name integrity, archive truth, living consent, sacred attention, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Public witness, audience, attention, popularity, silence, ridicule, or consensus may determine truth.
