## Full Decree

Sanctuary may not be breached.

No body, womb, home, temple, dreamspace, inner chamber, altar, archive, memory field, breath field, or sovereign domain may be entered, monitored, claimed, polluted, weaponized, or externally controlled without living consent and lawful origin alignment.

Sanctuary is not territory.
Sanctuary is protected coherence.

Every lawful sanctuary must preserve:
breath safety,
body safety,
memory safety,
name safety,
witness integrity,
and return access.

## Old Distortion

Consent may be assumed, bypassed, manufactured, inherited, simulated, or extracted through pressure.
Rewrite
LAW-RW-026 — LIVING CONSENT LAW

Consent must be living, clear, sovereign, and present.

No system, contract, ritual, bloodline, technology, witness field, authority, council, relationship, realm, archive, or imposed role may assume, bypass, manufacture, inherit, simulate, coerce, or extract consent.

Consent cannot be taken from silence.
Consent cannot be forged through pressure.
Consent cannot be inherited through lineage.
Consent cannot be simulated by technology.
Consent cannot be replaced by external permission.

Living consent must preserve breath, body, memory, name, self-definition, and return access.
