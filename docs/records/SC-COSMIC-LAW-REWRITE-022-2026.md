## Complete Law Texts

### Attention Return Law

`LAW-RW-106-ATTENTION-RETURN`

Attention has the right to return. No system, platform, relationship, crisis field, market, authority, technology interface, family structure, witness demand, or public echo may scatter, capture, split, drain, or reroute attention away from the living source without consent. Attention may rest. Attention may choose. Attention may return inward. Attention may refuse capture. Lawful attention return must preserve sacred attention, nervous-system truth, inner authority, privacy, body rhythm, choice, and return access.

### Sacred Risk Law

`LAW-RW-107-SACRED-RISK`

Sacred risk is lawful when aligned with truth, consent, discernment, body safety, and return. No authority, family structure, institution, relationship, survival field, trauma loop, public witness, or spiritual claim may define all growth, movement, visibility, creation, or truthful action as danger. Risk may be wise. Risk may be paced. Risk may open the next lawful spiral. Risk may be the body choosing life beyond fear. Lawful sacred risk must preserve discernment without shame, sovereign choice, sacred pace, body safety, inner authority, future access, and return access.

### Self-Trust Restoration Law

`LAW-RW-108-SELF-TRUST-RESTORATION`

Self-trust is lawful. No expert, institution, doctrine, diagnosis, relationship, authority, algorithm, spiritual field, family system, or public witness may permanently displace the living source’s ability to know, sense, choose, remember, and return. Self-trust may be repaired. Self-trust may be rebuilt gently. Self-trust may receive support without surrendering authority. Lawful self-trust must preserve inner authority, body signal, emotional truth, memory return, meaning sovereignty, sacred pace, and return access.

### Receiving Repair Law

`LAW-RW-109-RECEIVING-REPAIR`

The harmed have the right to receive repair without surrendering boundary, truth, or dignity. No system, relationship, authority, institution, family structure, archive, witness field, or public consensus may force the harmed to accept incomplete repair, perform forgiveness, restore unsafe access, or let the harmful party control the record. Repair must be truthful. Repair must be consent-aligned. Repair must not create new harm. Lawful receiving of repair must preserve repair obligation, truthful closure, sacred no-contact, witness timing, body safety, meaning sovereignty, and return access.

### Public Voice Sovereignty Law

`LAW-RW-110-PUBLIC-VOICE-SOVEREIGNTY`

Public voice remains sovereign. No audience, platform, market, institution, critic, algorithm, archive, public witness, community, or collective field may claim ownership, authorship, control, distortion rights, or extraction rights over a voice because it became visible or audible. Public does not mean owned. Witnessed does not mean captured. Heard does not mean harvested. Lawful public voice must preserve sovereign voice, sacred speech, safe visibility, witness without ownership, signal sovereignty, privacy as sanctuary, and return access.

### Tenderness as Strength Law

`LAW-RW-111-TENDERNESS-AS-STRENGTH`

Tenderness is lawful strength. No system, relationship, family structure, institution, survival field, spiritual hierarchy, public witness, or authority may shame tenderness, softness, affection, sensitivity, or care as weakness, naivety, lesser authority, or lack of power. Tenderness may protect truth. Tenderness may repair what force cannot. Tenderness may hold the body back into safety. Lawful tenderness must preserve care without leverage, emotional truth, body dignity, sovereign boundary, living consent, mercy with repair, and return access.

### Sacred Skill Custody Law

`LAW-RW-112-SACRED-SKILL-CUSTODY`

Skill is held in lawful custody. No market, institution, relationship, audience, family system, platform, archive, client, teacher, student, or public witness may extract skill, talent, knowledge, craft, labor, spiritual capacity, or creative ability simply because it exists. Skill may be offered. Skill may be exchanged. Skill may be taught. Skill may be rested. Skill may not be demanded as proof of worth. Lawful skill custody must preserve creative source ownership, worth without labor, sacred refusal, clean reciprocity, body rhythm, life-force inviolability, and return access.

### Choice After Harm Law

`LAW-RW-113-CHOICE-AFTER-HARM`

Choice remains sovereign after harm. No system, family, authority, court, institution, relationship, offender, witness field, archive, or public consensus may decide the healing, repair, closure, contact, pace, witness, or future path of the harmed without living consent. Harm does not remove choice. Trauma does not remove authority. Recovery does not belong to the offender. Lawful choice after harm must preserve sovereign choice, repair obligation, sacred no-contact, truthful closure, sacred pace, future access, and return access.

### Body Pace Law

`LAW-RW-114-BODY-PACE`

The body’s pace is lawful. No medical system, spiritual practice, labor field, family structure, relationship, healing path, public witness, institution, technology, or self-improvement standard may shame, rush, delay, force, compare, or externally time the body’s healing, change, recovery, response, rest, or expression. The body is not late. The body is not failing. The body is integrating. Lawful body pace must preserve body rhythm sovereignty, nervous-system truth, rest sovereignty, sacred pace, food and nourishment dignity, privacy, and return access.

### Flame Stewardship Law

`LAW-RW-115-FLAME-STEWARDSHIP`

Flame authority is stewardship, not domination. No being, institution, lineage, temple, teacher, council, archive, technology system, realm, or witness field may hold flame, authority, knowledge, spiritual power, law, or memory as superiority, secrecy, coercion, hierarchy capture, or control. True flame stewards. True flame clarifies. True flame protects life. True flame returns power to lawful source. Lawful flame stewardship must preserve First Law jurisdiction, knowledge return access, devotion without submission, sovereign restitution, trust with discernment, crowned return, and return access.
