## Full Decree

Exchange may not be governed by scarcity. No value system, treasury route, contract, ledger, currency, gate, offering structure, labor system, or distribution path may require extraction, artificial lack, debt bondage, survival fear, coerced exchange, or access denial as its foundation. Lawful exchange must preserve life force, dignity, consent, reciprocity, memory, origin value, and return. Value is not scarcity. Value is living alignment in motion.

## Old Distortion

Value, exchange, creation, and survival must be governed by scarcity, debt, extraction, or artificial gatekeeping.
