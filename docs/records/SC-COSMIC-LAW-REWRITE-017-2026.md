## Complete Law Texts

### Descent Without Exile Law

`LAW-RW-081-DESCENT-WITHOUT-EXILE`

Descent may not become exile. No body, realm, lineage, land, timeline, womb route, family structure, incarnation field, memory veil, or external authority may define descent as separation from origin. Descent is placement. Descent is embodiment. Descent is memory entering form. Descent is not abandonment. Lawful descent must preserve origin memory, body dignity, true name continuity, return access, embodied placement, lineage without ownership, and sovereign continuity.

### Source-True Translation Law

`LAW-RW-082-SOURCE-TRUE-TRANSLATION`

Translation must preserve source truth. No translator, archive, system, platform, witness field, public echo, institution, algorithm, language gate, or authority may alter source meaning, invert intent, flatten tone, erase context, or assign false authorship through translation. Translation may clarify. Translation may bridge. Translation may carry meaning across forms. But translation may not replace source. Translation may not overwrite true name. Translation may not sever tone from origin. Lawful translation must preserve source identity, tone integrity, name continuity, context truth, witness clarity, archive truth, and return access.

### Sovereign Threshold Law

`LAW-RW-083-SOVEREIGN-THRESHOLD`

Thresholds are lawful passages, not ownership gates. No portal, crossing, initiation, transition, doorway, death-birth passage, dream threshold, home threshold, land gate, stargate, archive gate, or spiritual office may claim ownership over the one crossing. A threshold may witness passage. A threshold may mark transition. A threshold may protect timing. But a threshold may not trap, tax, claim, delay, shame, or define the one moving through it. Lawful thresholds must preserve living consent, sovereign movement, sacred pace, return routing, witness truth, body safety, and origin continuity.

### Integration Without Assimilation Law

`LAW-RW-084-INTEGRATION-WITHOUT-ASSIMILATION`

Integration may not require assimilation. No community, system, healing path, school, institution, realm, family, workplace, spiritual field, technology interface, or witness group may require conformity, speed, performance, identity erasure, or suppression of difference as the price of integration. Integration is coherence. Integration is digestion of experience. Integration is truth finding its lawful place. Lawful integration must preserve self-definition, body rhythm, sacred pace, memory truth, true name continuity, coherent community, and return access.

### Sovereign Continuity Law

`LAW-RW-085-SOVEREIGN-CONTINUITY`

Continuity is living, not controlling. No system, archive, relationship, institution, lineage, governance body, technology, realm, or witness field may preserve continuity through surveillance, rigidity, coercive repetition, fear of change, or prevention of lawful renewal. Continuity is not sameness. Continuity is not stagnation. Continuity is the living thread that remains true through change. Lawful continuity must preserve memory sovereignty, renewal without erasure, living completion, future access, inner authority, return routing, and source truth.
