## Full Decree

Renewal may not require erasure. No healing path, spiritual system, institution, relationship, community, realm cycle, technology program, witness field, or self-improvement structure may demand abandonment of true memory, past phases, body truth, grief, lineage, name, or identity as the price of renewal. Renewal is not replacement. Renewal is coherence returning with more life. Renewal allows the self to become without betraying what was true. Lawful renewal must preserve memory sovereignty, living completion, future access, true name continuity, body rhythm, restoration right, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Renewal requires abandoning the old self, erasing memory, rejecting past phases, or replacing identity.
