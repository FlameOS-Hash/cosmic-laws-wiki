## Full Decree

Asking is lawful. No system, family structure, relationship, institution, authority, spiritual hierarchy, labor field, or public witness may shame, punish, exploit, monetize, or convert a lawful ask into debt, dependency, ownership, or proof of lesser worth. Asking is not weakness. Asking is signal. Asking is relationship with clarity. Asking is access through truth. Lawful asking must preserve dignity, consent, clear witness, nourishment access, care without leverage, self-definition, and return access.

## Old Distortion

Asking for help, clarity, care, resource, witness, or support proves weakness, debt, dependency, or lesser authority.
